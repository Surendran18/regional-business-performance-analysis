Findings

This analysis has been made on the basis of the completed orders and the significant months of the years 2024 and 2025.

The majority of the sales were generated by the Laptop and Smartphone categories. These categories contributed significantly to the sales in the important months analyzed, with July 2025 generating the highest completed sales among the significant months.

The North region ranks second in overall realized sales, completed sales, completed orders, customer total, and total units sold compared with the other regions. Despite ranking second in completed sales, North has the highest Average Order Value, which indicates that its completed sales were supported by a higher value per order. North also ranks fourth in Average Items per Order, showing that its sales were not mainly driven by having the highest number of items per order. Therefore, the higher sales value in the North region was supported more by the higher value of each order than by the number of items contained in each order.
