Findings

This analysis has been made under the criteria of significant sales and completed orders.

In the significant months analyzed, Laptop was a common contributing category, with strong contributions from products such as Creator Laptop, Ultrabook, Budget Laptop, Performance Laptop, and Business Laptop. In November 2025, Kitchen Appliances also became a significant contributing category.

The East region ranks third compared with the other regions in completed orders, customer total, and total units sold. However, it ranks fifth in both overall realized sales and completed sales and has the sixth-highest Average Order Value.

East has the highest Average Items per Order compared with the other regions, which means the region sold the highest average number of products in each order. However, this higher average number of items per order did not result in higher revenue, as East had the lowest Average Order Value. Therefore, the sales performance of East was not supported by a high value per order, despite having the highest average number of products sold per order.
