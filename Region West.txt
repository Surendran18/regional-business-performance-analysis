Findings

This analysis has been taken under the criteria of completed order sales and significant months.

The significant categories contributed a major part of the completed sales in the analyzed months, while the contributions from other categories in the remaining months were more shared and less significant.

The common category in this region is Laptop, which has generated more revenue comparatively.

The West region ranks third in overall realized sales and completed sales compared with the other regions. It ranks second in Average Order Value and second in Average Items per Order, showing that the region has a relatively high value per order and a relatively high number of products per order. However, it ranks fourth in customer total and total units sold and fifth in completed orders. Therefore, the lower completed-order volume limited the overall sales performance, even though the region had a higher value per order and higher average items per order.
